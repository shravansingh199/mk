A Sweet Confession

You know yeh jo bhi main likhta hoon
Yeh ek bahaana bhi hota hai
Tumse baatein karne ka..

So, here goes on more:
Nahi chahiye woh lamha
Jisme tumhara khayaal na ho,
Na chaahat hai us pal ki
Jahan tum exist na karo
Tum kya jaano yeh jahaan kitna roshan hai
Tumhare pal bhar thehar jaane se,
Kuch lamha ruk jaane se,
Kuch pal sang bitaane se
The moments:

They keep replaying when you're gone,
Bringing you back in my thoughts,
Missing you until you return again,
Giving me precious moments, more than I ever thought!
