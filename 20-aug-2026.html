She's happiness ☺

On the 20th of August 2026, in the very early hours before morning, at exactly 1:31 AM, she called my name for the very first time ever. It was one of the most special moments of its kind. Hearing my name from her, the person I admire, indeed, felt precious.

I used to wonder if I'd hear her say my name at all. She rarely sent voice messages, just one early on...

Things are different now, except she's as sweet as ever, and I get to hear her far more than I imagined. Back then though, I didn't even think much about that. I only wondered if I'd be able to hear her again.

I'm grateful she came back, and for letting me live these forever-special moments.

Today, she sounded as sweet as always, even though she had a cold. I noticed a faint change in the texture of her voice because of it after becoming aware that she wasn't well.

I barely slept, maybe three hours. I remember checking the time at 4:10, then waking around 7:30, restless in between. But why? Was it happiness from talking to her? Eagerness to wake up to this reality, which still feels dreamlike because of her? Maybe both!

Either way, the happiness she brings me is unfathomable.
